Readme.txt

Welcome to Print95. Run a print shop, from one desktop printer up to presses, binders and trucks. Keep your income above rent and upkeep, or you go bankrupt.

Start by hand: take a Blank Sheet from the Paper Supply, load the Printer, staple the print into a Zine, and drop it in the Outbox for $10.

Controls

  • Tap a machine's IN or OUT tray, or its middle, to use it.
  • Drag machines and belts to move them. Drag open floor to look around.
  • Pinch or scroll to zoom. Press F to see the whole floor.
  • Start menu: belts, splitters, bridges, the Shop and the Ledger.
  • Right-click goes back to Select.
  • Belts unlock once you've sold 20 Zines. Machines connect at the triangles on their trays.
  • Your shop saves itself in this browser. The Start menu can also save it to a file.

Grow: automate with belts, buy licenses for roll paper and production equipment, then run rolls through presses, sheeters and binders to pallets and trucks.
